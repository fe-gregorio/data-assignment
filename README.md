README

For this assigntment, I am analyzing a participant-profile table from Baldwin and Ripley’s (2020) qualitative study of other-than-human identity called “Exploring Other-Than-Human Identity:A Narrative Approach to Otherkin, Therianthropes, and Vampires”

The study can be found [here](https://www.qualitativesociologyreview.org/ENG/Volume54/QSR_16_3_Baldwin_Ripley.pdf). 

It summarizes the 24 interview participants by gender, identity, community affiliation (Otherkin, Therian, or Vampire) and whether they self-identified as belonging to an older generation. I added passages of the study with quotes from interviews made with the participants to the spreadsheet used to generate the data.

The interviewees were 24 people, who were found through posts and contacts on websites and Facebook pages, plus word-of-mouth referrals from earlier participants.
